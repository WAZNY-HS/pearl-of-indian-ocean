Pearl of Indian Ocean

Sri Lanka is often called the Pearl of the Indian Ocean, and for good reason. On one small island you can climb ancient rock fortresses, ride a train through tea country, spot leopards in the wild and relax on golden beaches.

Pearl of Indian Ocean is a travel guide that brings the island's best destinations together in one place. Browse must-see places, compare travel packages for every province and plan your trip with our booking form.

Whether you want culture, wildlife, adventure or the sea, start exploring and find your own pearl.
